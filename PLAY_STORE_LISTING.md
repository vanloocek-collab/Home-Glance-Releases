## English (United States)

### Short description

Weather, calendar, next alarm and smart details in one clean Android widget.

Google Play limit: 80 characters.

### Full description

Home Glance brings the information you need to your Android home screen in a clean, customizable widget inspired by the simplicity of At a Glance.

See your current weather, upcoming calendar events, next alarm and useful contextual information without opening multiple apps.

**Designed for your home screen**

- Current weather with automatic or manual location
- Upcoming calendar events with flexible filtering
- Next alarm displayed alongside the date
- Smart weather insights for rain, snow, wind, heat and other conditions
- Independent scrolling for date/alarm, weather and calendar lines
- Adjustable scrolling intervals
- Custom text size, alignment and date formatting
- Material You and Liquid Glass widget backgrounds
- Adjustable wallpaper visibility
- Support for different widget sizes and Android launchers
- 11 supported languages

Home Glance is designed to stay simple on the outside while offering extensive customization when you want it.

The project is actively developed and currently available as a public test release. Feedback and bug reports are welcome.

Home Glance is an independent project and is not affiliated with Google, Pixel At a Glance or Another Widget.

Google Play limit: 4000 characters.
