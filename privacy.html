PRIVACY

USEFUL DATA. MINIMUM DRAMA.

Home Glance has no account system, advertising SDKs or behavioral tracking.

Effective date: September 24, 2026

Summary

Most settings, calendar information and alarm information are processed locally on the device. Weather features require a network connection and may send selected coordinates to the weather provider.

Weather and location

Automatic weather location uses Android approximate location. Coordinates are stored locally and sent over HTTPS to Open-Meteo to retrieve weather data.

Calendar

Calendar access is optional. Event data is read locally and is not sent to a Home Glance server.

Next alarm

Next-alarm information is read locally from Android and is not sent to a Home Glance server.

Local storage

Widget options, language, selected calendars and weather options are stored locally in app preferences.

Advertising and tracking

Home Glance does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs or behavioral tracking, and does not sell user data.
