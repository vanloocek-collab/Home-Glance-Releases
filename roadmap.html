FUTURE DEVELOPMENT

WHAT COMES NEXT.

First: two new dedicated widgets. Then: three phases that bring Home Glance to full Another Widget feature parity and beyond.

01 / Music widget

A dedicated media widget with current track and artist, artwork where available, media controls and player filtering.

02 / Wireless headphones

Connection and battery status, L/R/case levels where exposed, plus ANC / Transparency / Off controls on supported devices.

03 / Phase 1 — Layout, typography and actions

Expanded text, font, color, opacity, spacing, alignment and background controls, plus configurable tap actions for every major widget area.

04 / Phase 2 — Clock, calendar and weather

Bring the remaining Another Widget clock, calendar and weather options into the current Home Glance design, including deeper filters, time-zone controls, refresh settings and provider options.

05 / Phase 3 — Smart Glance

A priority-based provider system for alarms, battery, media, notifications, greetings, custom notes, steps and calendar events.

No fixed dates

This is a development direction, not a release-date promise. Compatibility and reliability fixes can move ahead of roadmap work when needed.
