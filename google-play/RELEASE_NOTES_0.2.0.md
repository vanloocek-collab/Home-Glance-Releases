# Home Glance v0.2.0 — Google Play release notes

## English

Home Glance v0.2.0 improves widget customization and everyday usability.

- Refined Home Glance app interface
- Material You and Liquid Glass widget backgrounds
- Independent scrolling controls for date/alarm, weather and calendar lines
- Improved long-text handling
- Next-alarm support
- Weather and calendar customization
- Multiple language improvements
- General fixes and polish
